PROPERTY GUIDANCE

Protect your investment.

Important considerations every property buyer, seller, landlord and investor should understand before making a real estate decision.

Before making a property payment, ensure the property's ownership and available title documentation are properly reviewed and verified.

Visit and inspect a property before committing to a transaction. Consider the location, access, surroundings and condition of the property.

Make sure important property terms, responsibilities, payments and agreements are properly documented before completing a transaction.

Compare the property's location, features, condition and current market conditions before deciding whether the asking price makes sense for your goals.

Understand the full cost of a property transaction, including agreed payments, professional fees, documentation costs and other applicable charges.

Before completing a property transaction, confirm that the necessary documents, keys, possession details and agreed handover requirements are properly handled.
